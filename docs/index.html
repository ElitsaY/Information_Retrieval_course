Recommended projects

SemEval-2027 shared tasks

For the course project we recommend taking part in a task from SemEval-2027, the 21st International Workshop on Semantic Evaluation. Each task is already defined by its organisers and comes with its own data and evaluation, so you can focus on building and analysing a system instead of collecting a dataset.

  • Teams: of at most 2 students.
  • Choose a task: the 11 tasks cover reasoning and inference, lexical semantics, cross-lingual and cultural understanding, stereotypes, framing and sentiment, and AI agents and tools.
  • Data and evaluation: provided by each task's organisers; see the task pages and their contact / mailing-list links.
