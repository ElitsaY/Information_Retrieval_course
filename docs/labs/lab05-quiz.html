Assumptions unless stated otherwise

  • Rankings go from rank 1 (the top) downward. R means relevant, N not relevant.
  • For binary metrics on graded judgments, a grade of 1 or more counts as relevant.
  • Average Precision divides by all relevant documents for the query, including the ones that were not retrieved.
  • A query with no relevant result retrieved has Reciprocal Rank 0.
  • DCG uses the gain \(2^{rel} - 1\) and the discount \(\log_2(i + 1)\).
  • Round numbers to three decimals; small rounding differences are accepted.

How answers are scored

  • Points: questions 1–20 are worth 3 points, the four calculations 4, and the three case scenarios (several questions about one situation) 8.
  • Single choice: choose one option.
  • Numbers: type a number such as 0.571 into the box; a question with several numbers splits its points between them. For accuracy, a percentage such as 99.4% also works.
  • Rows: one choice per row; each correct row earns its share of the points.
  • Select all that apply: scores only when the selection is exactly right.
